Established 1990

Legacy in Every Thread.

Quality products. On-time delivery. Continuous improvement.

AD Textile was established in 1990 as a manufacturer and exporter of quality home textile products to the international market. Since 1992, we have exported our products worldwide, building lasting relationships with global buyers.

We are known for our quality products, on-time delivery, and continuous process improvement. Our contemporary infrastructure and well-groomed personnel work together to uphold the standards our international clients expect, shipment after shipment.

Environmental Care & CSR

Caring for our environment and our community.

Our environmental care program includes an Effluent Treatment Plant, a Reverse Osmosis Plant, and a Vibratory Shear Enhanced Process (VSEP) plant, and we use only Azo-free dyes across our dyeing operations. We believe industrial success is meaningless without ecological and social responsibility.
